MythicRep isn't a game you play instead of training. Logging real workouts is the whole point. Every base card comes in a woman's, a man's and a fantasy version, or use your own photo.

Features

The cards just make you want to come back.

Underneath the game is a real training log built for the gym floor.

01

Cards

Each movement is a card with its own sets, rep range and working weight. Squats, presses, pull-ups, runs, rower sprints, swings and more, or make your own.

02

Decks and a weekly schedule

Build a deck for each workout, set which days you train, and Today shows the deck that's up. Miss one and it's offered as a catch-up later in the week.

03

Progression that runs itself

Hit every set and the weight goes up next time. Miss once and it holds. Miss twice and it backs off a little so you can build again.

04

XP and levels

Every set, personal best and weight increase earns XP. Your cards level up, and so do you, with a title for every stage of the climb.

05

Quests

Optional multi-week programs with a weekly goal and a progress bar. Finish and claim your reward, or add a week if life got in the way.

06

Made for the gym floor

Big tap targets, a rest timer on your lock screen, supersets, a set timer for holds and intervals, and pounds or kilos, miles or kilometers.
