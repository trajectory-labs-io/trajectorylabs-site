Why DishDecoded

Calorie apps treat cooking like a chore. This one treats it like the point.

Most trackers are built for packaged food and takeout. If you cook, you shouldn't have to weigh every ingredient.

Typical trackers

  • Weigh and search every ingredient in your recipe.
  • Re-enter the same home-cooked meals over and over.
  • Recipes in one app, tracking in another.
  • Numbers with no story, so you do the interpreting.

DishDecoded

  • Import the recipe once and get per-serving nutrition instantly.
  • Cook it with step-by-step mode and automatic timers.
  • Log a serving in one tap. Your frequents learn your habits.
  • An AI recap every evening tells you how the day actually went.

Features

Built for the way you really eat.

Everything a home cook needs to track without the busywork.

01

Import any recipe

Paste a link or share straight from your browser. DishDecoded pulls out the ingredients, steps and per-serving nutrition from virtually any recipe site.

02

Cooking mode with auto-timers

Step-by-step cooking with timers found right in the recipe text, so "bake 15 minutes" becomes a one-tap timer. Log the meal the moment it's done.

03

AI daily recaps

Every evening, a personal recap of wins, watch-outs and one suggestion for tomorrow. Weekly and monthly recaps show the bigger picture.

04

Describe or snap to log

Type "two eggs and buttered toast" or snap a photo of your plate, and AI estimates the full nutrition.

05

Logging that learns you

Frequents that know breakfast from dinner, saved meals, barcode scanning and a food database tuned for real cooking.

06

Synced and safe

Your history, recipes and streaks sync to the cloud. New phone? Sign in and everything is right where you left it.

Privacy

Your data runs the app for you. That's it.

The short version from DishDecoded's privacy policy. Read the full privacy policy.

Not sold, no ads

Your personal data isn't sold or shared with advertisers, and there are no ads.

Not used to train AI

Food descriptions, meal photos and recipe pages are sent to an AI service to estimate nutrition. None of it is used to train AI models.

Photos aren't kept

A meal photo is processed to estimate nutrition and isn't stored on DishDecoded's servers.
