Features

Learn it, don't just memorize it.

Built for the exams you will actually sit, with the FCC's own questions and the real format.

01

Elements 1, 3 and 8

Element 1, basic radio law and operating practice, and Element 3, general radiotelephone, for the General Radiotelephone Operator License (GROL). Element 8, ship radar techniques, for the Ship Radar Endorsement.

02

An explanation for all 1,044 questions

Every question in the FCC's Element 1, 3 and 8 pools, word for word, with a plain-language explanation of why the right answer is right. The official figures are included, with pinch to zoom.

03

Practice exams in the real format

One question from each key topic, just like exam day: 24 with 18 to pass for Element 1, 100 with 75 for Element 3, 50 with 38 for Element 8. Change answers until you submit, then review what you missed.

04

Spaced repetition and Weak Spots

Questions you miss come back before you forget them, and a Weak Spots drill goes straight to the topics that need work.

05

Daily Check-In and awards

10 questions, about 3 minutes a day, with a streak to keep and an optional daily reminder. Earn awards like On Watch, Clean Sweep and Perfect Copy along the way.

06

Readiness you can see

Progress and exam readiness for each element, on iPhone and iPad, so you know when you are ready to book the exam.
