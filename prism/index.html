Features

The little things, done right.

Everything you need to keep track of the day, and nothing that gets in the way.

01

Today, at a glance

Everything overdue, due today and in focus, from every list, on one screen. Check things off right there, with undo if you tapped too soon.

02

Three kinds of lists

Task lists with due dates and reminders, checklists you clear in one tap, and routines that reset by hand or every day or week.

03

Quick to add

Natural dates like tomorrow, Friday or 3pm, and paste a whole list to turn each line into its own item.

04

Widgets

See what's due today on your home screen and lock screen without opening the app.

05

Profiles

Keep Personal and Work apart, each with its own lists, and drag lists and tasks into the order that works for you.

06

A daily check-in

An optional nudge each day to look over your lists, and a Log that keeps what you finished for 90 days.

Privacy

Your lists stay yours.

The short version from Prism's privacy policy. Read the full privacy policy.

Nothing collected

No sign-up or account. Prism doesn't send your lists or anything else to us or anyone else.

Stored on your device

Your lists, tasks and reminders live in the app on your phone, and reminders are scheduled right on the device.

Backed up to your own account

On iPhone and iPad, iCloud Backup keeps a copy in your own iCloud. On Android, your device backup can include Prism in your own Google account.
