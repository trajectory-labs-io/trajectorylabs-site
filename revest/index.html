Features

Three strategies, one clear score.

Built for your first rental or your fiftieth flip. Set your expense rates, financing terms and targets once, and every new analysis starts from them.

01

Rental analysis

Cash flow, cash-on-cash return, cap rate, DSCR, NOI and the 1% rule for buy-and-hold properties.

02

House flip analysis

Net profit, ROI, annualized ROI and the maximum allowable offer, so you know your number before you bid.

03

BRRRR analysis

Buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat: see the cash you put in, how much comes back at the refinance, the equity created and the cash flow after.

04

A Deal Score you can check

Every deal gets a score out of 100 with a plain-English summary, and you can see exactly how it's calculated.

05

Save, sort and compare

Save deals with photos, synced across iPhone, iPad and the web. Free accounts keep 3 deals. Pro keeps them all and ranks up to 3 side by side.

06

Projections and PDF reports

PDF reports to share with partners, lenders or your agent. Pro adds 5-year projections from your own appreciation and rent growth assumptions.
