Features

Three strategies, one clear score.

Built for your first rental or your fiftieth flip. Set default preferences to match your market, then run the numbers.

01

Rental analysis

Cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return and DSCR for buy-and-hold properties.

02

House flip analysis

Net profit, ROI and the maximum allowable offer, so you know your number before you bid.

03

BRRRR analysis

Buy, rehab, rent, refinance: see the equity created, the cash returned and how the property performs after the refinance.
