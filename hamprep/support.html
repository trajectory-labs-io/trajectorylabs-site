Common questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

What does it cost?

The download is free, and chapter 1 of each license, one full practice exam per license, the Daily Net Check-In and all reference cards stay free. The Technician License and the General License are $4.99 each, or get both with the All Licenses Bundle for $7.99. Every purchase is one-time, there is no subscription, and Family Sharing is included.

Which question pools are included?

The Technician 2026-2030 pool (February 2026 errata) and the General 2023-2027 pool (6th errata, February 2026).

Does my progress sync?

Yes, if iCloud is on for your Apple Account. HamPrep keeps your progress, streaks and awards in your own iCloud so they follow you to your other devices. No HamPrep account is needed. You can turn it off in Settings, Backup.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every question, explanation, figure and reference card is on your device, so you can study without a connection.

Found a wrong answer?

Tap "Report a problem with this question" under the explanation, or email us the question ID (for example T1A01). We check every report against the official pool.
