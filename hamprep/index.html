Features

Learn it, don't just memorize it.

Built for the exam you will actually sit, with the current pools and the real format.

01

Current question pools

Technician 2026-2030 with the February 2026 errata, valid from July 1, 2026. General 2023-2027 with the 6th errata, withdrawn questions removed.

02

A clear explanation for all 832 questions

Plain language, worked math where it matters, and the FCC rule behind the answer. Official figures T-1, T-2, T-3 and G7-1 with pinch to zoom.

03

Practice exams in the real format

35 questions drawn one from each group, 26 to pass, a 30 minute pacing timer, and results broken down by chapter.

04

Spaced repetition

Questions you miss come back right when you are about to forget them, until you get them right for good.

05

Daily Net Check-In and awards

10 questions, about 3 minutes a day, with a streak to keep alive. Earn Worked All Chapters, Pileup Buster, Elmer, 73 and more. Passed? Share an "I passed" card with your new call sign.

06

Handy reference and exam finder

Band privileges for Technician and General, key formulas, the phonetic alphabet and Q signals. Find an exam session near you and know what to bring.
