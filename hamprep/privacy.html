Privacy

HamPrep Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026. Published by Trajectory Labs.

What we collect

HamPrep does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and sends nothing to Trajectory Labs.

Your progress and iCloud

Your study progress is saved on your device. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, HamPrep can also keep a copy of your progress, streaks and awards in your own iCloud (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it appears on your other devices. That copy lives in your iCloud account, is managed by Apple, and is never sent to us. You can turn this off at any time in HamPrep under Settings, Backup.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens an email in your own mail app that you review before sending. We only receive what you send.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Questions

Questions: email support@trajectorylabs.io.
